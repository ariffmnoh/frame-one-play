How to play

Test is the open camera sandbox. Cafe is a six-minute date: take photos for five requests, then choose an album to show Eliana. 65% passes.

Mouse and keyboard: click the picture to look with the mouse · F raise / lower the camera · left click focus · right click or Space take a photo · 1–8 aperture, shutter, ISO, exposure · L lens · T stand / sit · B album · Esc pause.

Touch: drag to look, tap to focus, the big round button takes the photo; the left stick walks or leans.
